Project overview

Parspetrox supplies polymers, chemicals, oil derivatives and bitumen to buyers on four continents. The website turns a technical catalog into a clear buying path: find your grade, verify the quality, request a quote.

English-first, document-driven and built to answer a buyer’s questions before they ask.

01 - About the project

The site is organised around four divisions — Polymers, Chemicals, Oil Derivatives and Bitumen — each with grade-level products, so a buyer can start from the exact grade they need.

Trust is built into the structure: quality verification lot by lot, a world map of supplied countries, logistics and team content, exhibition coverage and a blog of practical market notes.

From “what grade do I need?” to a quote request in a few clicks.

  • 01 — Pick a division or search by grade
  • 02 — Review the product and its documents
  • 03 — Read market notes and quality information
  • 04 — Request a quote
