Project overview

Naghshineh is an engineering group offering UTM mapping, topographic surveying and land-registration services. The website explains each service clearly and turns visitors into free-consultation requests.

01 - About the project

Eight services — from UTM maps in the Shamim system and as-built drawings to topographic surveying, parcel positioning, subdivision and aerial-photo interpretation — each get an illustrated card with a clear explanation.

A completed-projects section with a project map, a collaboration process, certificates, a team page and a news-and-articles section add credibility, and a free-consultation form sits within reach throughout.

Complex services, explained simply — and one click from a consultation.
