Project overview

Faramehr Alborz is an automotive-parts manufacturer specialising in precision machining. The website presents its product groups, process, clients and quality certifications in a clear, industrial design.

Blue, steel and white — a design that puts the parts and the proof of quality first.

01 - About the project

The site is organised around three product groups — engine parts, power-transmission parts and cooling-system parts — followed by a visual catalog of the machined components themselves.

A short “how we work” process, key numbers, client logos and ISO and IQNet-style certifications build confidence, while a FAQ and clear contact details make it easy to start a conversation.

A catalog first — with the proof of quality right behind it.
